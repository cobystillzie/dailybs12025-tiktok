Privacy Policy

DailyBS12025 Uploader is a private desktop automation tool used only by the owner of the @DailyBS12025 TikTok account.

The tool uses TikTok OAuth only so the owner can authorize uploads to the owner's own TikTok account. OAuth tokens are intended to be stored locally on the owner's computer and are not sold, shared, or used for third-party accounts.

The tool does not collect public user data, does not provide analytics services to other users, and does not allow third-party users to log in.

DailyBS12025 Uploader

Official app website

DailyBS12025 Uploader is a private desktop automation tool used by the owner of the @DailyBS12025 TikTok account to upload locally produced short-form vertical clips to that same account.

The tool is not offered to the public, does not publish for third parties, and does not support multiple TikTok accounts.
